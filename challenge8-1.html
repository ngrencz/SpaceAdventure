Asteroid Outpost Escape Room
Mission Brief:
You are the replacement crew, rocketed out to Mining/Science Outpost IX in the mid-belt. En route, the outpost mysteriously lost contact with all outside comms.

On arrival, the airlock is sealed by a multi-dimensional lock. Only fragmented clues remain, buried in the outpost’s math-based control systems.

Each team must solve their segment’s puzzle to unlock their part of the airlock. Once solved, unite to help other teams crack their codes and save the outpost.
Hint: These puzzles will require teamwork and sharp math skills!
Group 1: Slope Sequence Override

The engineers left equations as code. Solve for the slope (m) of each equation, and enter them in order, separated by commas.

  • y = 2x + 4
  • y = -3x + 7
  • y = x - 5
Group 2: Y-Intercept Subsystem

Decrypted files show equations—the y-intercepts (b) are the code. Enter the y-intercepts in order, comma-separated.

  • y = 4x + 2
  • y = -x + 6
  • y = 3x - 1
Group 3: Slope-Intercept Cipher

Maintenance logs need updating. Write each line in y = mx + b form (comma-separated, no spaces, use 'y=').

  • Slope: 5, y-intercept: -2
  • Slope: -1, y-intercept: 4
Group 4: Linear Discovery

Solve for x in each equation. Enter your answers, comma-separated.

  • 2x = 8
  • 3x + 1 = 7
  • x - 5 = 3
Group 5: System Restore Core

Critical control system glitches!
Solve the following system for x and y. Enter as x,y.

  • 2x + y = 9
    x - y = 1

All Segments Unlocked!

The airlock opens with a hiss of escaping pressurized gas. You have entered the outpost.
But what awaits in the next chamber? Future challenges are being designed!
