Asteroid Outpost IX

Crew Mission Briefing

Welcome, replacement crew, to Asteroid Outpost IX.

Purpose: Outpost IX is home to both gritty mining operations and cutting-edge scientific research. Your original mission: relieve the current crew, analyze new ore samples, and calibrate the deep-space sensor array.

Situation: Midway through your launch, all communications from Outpost IX stopped. HQ detected one last encoded transmission—heavy interference, but traces suggested serious systems trouble.

Backstory: Outpost IX runs on math-based control locks, a security measure built by its eccentric designer, Dr. Pascal "Numbers" Euler. Every critical system is protected by live algebraic equations to ensure only engineers with proven math skills can access vital controls.

Now, there are no instructions—only scattered equations remain.
Your task: crack the math clues, restore access, and save the outpost!
